## Contradictory finding and correction

The first depth test exposed a P0 benchmark correctness issue: when a matched-flow date was a weekday with a missing exact benchmark close, the engine could shift the flow silently to the next available close and return `PASS`.

The behavior originated from the causal next-close mechanism introduced in v5.1 PR #23 for genuine non-session dates. The engine had no way to distinguish a non-session date from an interior provider-data gap.

Correction: PR #65, merged before the depth suite.

Conservative rule after the correction:

- exact flow-date close present -> use it;
- weekend flow -> causal next available close is allowed;
- weekday flow with no exact close -> benchmark is `N/A`;
- no future price is used.

Known limitation: a weekday Xetra holiday without an exact close is also conservatively `N/A`, because the local replay engine has no authoritative exchange-calendar dependency. This may reduce benchmark availability but cannot silently manufacture a price.

9. import the backup;
